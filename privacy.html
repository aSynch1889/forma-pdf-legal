Privacy Policy for Forma PDF

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data Processed Locally

Forma PDF is built on an on-device architecture. Your scanned documents, images, signatures, and OCR recognition results are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers or third-party tracking services without your explicit action.

2. Camera and Photos Permissions

Camera and Photo Library permissions are exclusively used for document scanning, edge detection, and image importing.

3. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

All financial transactions are handled securely through Apple's StoreKit 2 and App Store ecosystem. We do not collect or store credit card details.

4. Contact

If you have any questions, please contact support@evolly.app.
