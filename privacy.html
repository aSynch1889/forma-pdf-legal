Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Forma PDF is a document scanner for iPhone and iPad. This policy describes how the app handles information. We designed the product so that your documents do not need to leave your device for core scanning, OCR, and signing.

1. Data processed on your device

Scanned pages, imported photos, PDF files, OCR text, signatures, folders, and app settings are stored in the app sandbox on your device. We do not operate a cloud document store for this app. We do not sell personal data.

  • Documents are not uploaded by default.
  • There is no account required to scan or save files.
  • If you share a PDF through the iOS share sheet, that action is initiated by you and handled by Apple or the destination you choose.

2. Camera and Photos

Camera access is used only to capture documents, codes, and related scan modes. Photo Library access is used only when you import an existing image. We do not use these permissions for advertising or background collection.

3. Subscriptions and App Store payments

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your credit card number. Apple may provide purchase status (for example, whether Pro is active) so the app can unlock features and restore purchases.

4. Text and math tools

OCR and local text helpers run on device. They summarize or process text already on your device. They do not send your documents to a cloud model unless a future version discloses that change in this policy and in the app.

Questions? Email support@evolly.app. We will update this page if our practices change.
